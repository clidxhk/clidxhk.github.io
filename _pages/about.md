<h2 style="color: #2e7d32; margin-top: 0;">Education & Research</h2>

<p>I am currently a Ph.D. student in the Department of Chemistry at the <strong style="color: #2e7d32;">Hong Kong University of Science and Technology</strong>. I completed both my Bachelor's and Master's degrees at the <strong style="color: #2e7d32;">Southern University of Science and Technology</strong>.</p>

<p>My recent research focuses on photochemistry and its applications in the biomedical field. My key interests include:</p>
<ul style="color: #1b5e20;">
  <li>Advanced imaging technologies</li>
  <li>Remote phototherapy</li>
  <li>Excited-state molecules</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="padding: 20px; margin: 25px 0; background-color: #e3f2fd; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 5px solid #2196f3;">
<h2 style="color: #1565c0; margin-top: 0;">Vision & Direction</h2>

<p>In my ongoing research, I am dedicated to leveraging <strong style="color: #1565c0;">data-driven molecular engineering</strong> to accelerate advancements in biomedicine while exploring the potential of artificial intelligence in chemistry and biomedical sciences.</p>
</div>

<div style="padding: 20px; margin: 25px 0; background-color: #fff3e0; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 5px solid #ff9800;">
<h2 style="color: #e65100; margin-top: 0;">Research Philosophy</h2>

<p>My ultimate research goals are threefold:</p>

<ul style="color: #e65100;">

</ul>
